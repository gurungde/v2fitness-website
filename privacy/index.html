Privacy Notice

Module 01 privacy commitment: Growth and Maturation calculations run locally inside your web browser. V2Fitness does not collect, transmit, store or retain the child measurements, parent-height measurements or calculated results entered or generated in Module 01.

Information not collected by Module 01

The module is designed not to submit or retain the child's name, date of birth, age, sex, standing height, sitting height, weight, biological-parent heights, estimated adult height, maturity-offset result, PHV estimate or growth-stage result.

No account or embedded lead form

Module 01 does not require account registration and does not contain an embedded marketing or contact form. Contacting V2Fitness through the main website is a separate and voluntary action.

Standard technical website information

Like most hosted websites, Netlify or other hosting, security and network providers may automatically process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, device information and request time. These technical logs are not intended to contain the measurements or results entered into the calculator.

Information voluntarily submitted to V2Fitness

Where an adult submits a contact or enquiry form on the main V2Fitness website, V2Fitness may use the supplied contact information to respond to that request. Do not submit a child's sensitive health information or calculator results through a general enquiry form unless specifically requested and appropriate consent has been obtained.

Use by parents, guardians and coaches

Youth OS is intended to be used by a parent, legal guardian, authorised adult or appropriately responsible coach. The user is responsible for having suitable authority to enter information about a child.

Future features

If Youth OS later introduces user accounts, cloud storage, saved profiles, paid services, analytics involving calculator inputs or other forms of data processing, the privacy notice and consent process should be updated before those features are released.

Questions and requests

Questions about privacy, or requests relating to information voluntarily submitted to V2Fitness, can be made through the contact details on the main V2Fitness website.

Last updated: July 2026. This privacy notice is a practical website draft and should be reviewed by an Australian privacy or legal professional before commercial scale-up or the collection of personal or sensitive information.
