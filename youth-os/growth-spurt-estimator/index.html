How to measure sitting height

  1. Place a firm, flat box or bench against a wall.
  2. Have the child sit tall, looking straight ahead, with the back upright.
  3. Keep the thighs supported and feet still.
  4. Measure vertically from the sitting surface to the highest point of the head.
  5. Measure twice and use the closest repeatable value.
Do not measure from the floor. The box or bench height must be excluded.
